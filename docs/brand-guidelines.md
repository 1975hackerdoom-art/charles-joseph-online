# Catch the Beat brand direction

Reference: the supplied navy and gold CTB business card.

- Primary colors: navy #001B2E, gold #B68D53, off-white #FAFAFA.
- Supporting colors: pale gold #E5C991, warm paper #F0E7DA.
- Use the existing CTB monogram without redrawing or stretching it.
- Use the shared AIIcon component for crisp, rounded line icons. Match motifs
  to the service or action; use navy on light surfaces and pale gold on navy.
- Nunito for body text, headings, navigation, buttons and form fields.
- Use weight 600 (semibold) for H1 through H4, preserving the heading size hierarchy.
- Allura for handwritten script accents, including “Real perspective”.
- Body and form text remain at least 16px, scaling toward 18px on desktop.
- Navy banners use fine animated gold light ribbons and angular gold corners.
- Keep decorative artwork behind content, noninteractive and hidden from
  assistive technology. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Use navy text on gold buttons and light text on navy. Small gold text on
  light surfaces uses the darker gold-ink token for contrast.
- Contact email: digital@catchthebeat.ca. The older email on the reference
  card is not used. Newsletter: http://eepurl.com/joOFTE.

Implementation: app/brand.css, components/BrandHero.tsx,
components/FloatingSilhouettes.tsx and components/FlowArt.tsx.
